Regulator Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out output voltages, divider resistors and dissipations for an adjustable three-terminal regulator from the numbers you type: the 1.25 V reference (a named chart constant from the classic adjustable-regulator datasheet) times one plus the divider ratio for the output, the target back into a lower resistor, and the drop times the current for the heat. The resistors, voltages and currents are yours to measure or read off your own circuit and datasheet. It treats the reference as exactly 1.25 V and ignores the adjust-pin current, so it has not seen your regulator and does not know the tolerance, the dropout behavior, the thermal limits or the part variant, and it gives no verdict that a regulator circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The divider truth

What the pair sets: the reference times one plus the ratio of lower to upper.

The resistor truth

The lower resistor for the output you want, given the upper one.

The dissipation truth

The heat: the drop across the part times the current through it.
